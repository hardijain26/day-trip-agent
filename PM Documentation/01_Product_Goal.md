# ✈️ DayTrip Planner — PM Documentation

## 🎯 1. Product Goal

### Product

**DayTrip Planner**

### 🎯 Goal

Help users turn a vague day-trip intention into a personalized, practical, and budget-aware plan with minimal planning effort.

### 😕 User Problem

Users often know:

- 📍 Where they want to go
- 🧘 How they want to feel
- 🎨 What they are interested in
- 💰 Roughly how much they want to spend

But turning these preferences into an actual day plan still requires users to manually research and connect:

- 📍 Places to visit
- 🎯 Activities
- 🕐 Opening hours
- 🎟️ Events
- 🍽️ Food options
- 💵 Costs
- 🚗 Travel considerations
- 🗓️ How to structure the day

The problem is therefore not a lack of travel information.

> **The problem is the effort required to turn scattered information into a personalized, coherent decision.**

### 💡 Product Opportunity

Users can already find individual travel recommendations across search engines, maps, review platforms, and travel websites.

The opportunity for DayTrip Planner is to bridge the gap between:

**Information Discovery → Decision Making → Actionable Plan**

The agent can interpret the user's intent, research relevant information, curate the best-fit options, and organize them into a practical day-trip experience.

### 🚀 MVP Outcome

The agent should transform a request such as:

> "Plan a relaxing and artsy day trip near Sunnyvale, CA. Keep it affordable."

into:

- 🌅 A morning plan
- ☀️ An afternoon plan
- 🌆 An evening plan
- 📍 Specific places or activities
- 🔎 Current information where available
- 🎨 Recommendations aligned with the user's mood and interests
- 💰 Recommendations that respect the user's budget
- 🗺️ A coherent sequence that the user can actually follow

### 🧭 Product Principle

The product should optimize for:

**Relevant + Practical + Current + Budget-aware**

rather than simply generating a generic travel itinerary.

The ultimate product outcome is:

> **Reduce the user's planning effort while improving confidence in the resulting day-trip plan.**
